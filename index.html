Booking terms

  • Deposit: a 50% deposit, paid by the payment link we send you, confirms your booking. The rest is due on the day or before files are delivered.
  • Moving or cancelling: give us at least 48 hours' notice and we'll move your booking or refund the deposit. With less notice, the deposit is kept.
  • Changes to mixes and masters: one round of changes is included. A round is one set of notes sent together. Extra rounds are £25 each. New parts, re-recorded vocals or a different beat count as a new mix.
  • Artist development: £300 a month on a 3-month minimum, paid monthly in advance. Unused studio time doesn't roll over to the next month. Availability is limited. When it's full, you can join the waiting list and we'll message you when a place opens.

Privacy

How we handle the details you give us.

What The Audio Box does

The Audio Box is a music network for independent artists and creatives. We publish a weekly blog, spotlight artists and share what's moving in music.

What we collect

  • Mailing list: your email address, so we can send you the weekly blog. Our mailing list is run by beehiiv.
  • Enquiries: whatever you choose to send us on WhatsApp, by email or on Instagram.

Our promise

  • We only use your details to send you the newsletter and reply to you.
  • We never sell or share your details.
  • You can unsubscribe from any email with one click, and ask us to delete your details at any time.

Where it is kept

  • Mailing list emails are stored by beehiiv under their privacy policy.
  • Messages you send on WhatsApp or Instagram are kept by those apps under their own privacy rules.

Questions or deleting your details

Email us at theaudiobox.pr@gmail.com or message us on Instagram @theaudiobox_. If you are unhappy with how your details have been handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
